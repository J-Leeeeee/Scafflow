# Users and Stakeholders

## Primary User: The Student

**Who:** University students in rigorous STEM courses—initially ECE undergraduates taking circuit analysis or related classes.

**Situation:** They have assigned homework tied to a specific course. They may vary widely in prior preparation, attention patterns, confidence, and independence. Some need heavy structure; others need the system to get out of the way.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Understand what the problem is asking and how to approach it
- Get timely feedback on reasoning—not just a final grade
- Stay motivated when stuck without giving up or cheating the learning process
- Feel that the tool respects their agency (choice of method, pace, when to ask for help)

**Pain points today:**

- Generic office hours and static solutions do not match how they learn
- Public AI tools solve the problem but skip the thinking
- One static homework UI frustrates both beginners and advanced learners

---

## Secondary User: The Instructor / Teaching Team

**Who:** Course instructors, TAs, and instructional designers who assign homework and care about learning outcomes and academic integrity.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Assign problems that develop reasoning, not answer lookup
- Offer scalable support without normalizing answer shortcuts
- Understand whether students are learning or merely assisted
- Configure guardrails so AI help stays aligned with course pedagogy

**What they need from Scafflow:**

- Course-aligned problem assignments (same problems students would do anyway)
- Configurable scaffolding levels and support policies
- Visibility into how students engage (without surveillance that undermines trust)
- Confidence that the product supports—not replaces—their teaching goals

---

## Other Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
|-------------|----------|
| **University / IRB** | Student privacy, FERPA compliance, ethical use of learning data |
| **Researchers** | Evidence that adaptive scaffolding improves engagement and learning; valid study design |
| **Instructional designers** | Clear learner models, scaffold design, content authoring for multi-profile flows |

---

## User Context (Launch)

Initial launch targets integration with a **university ECE course** (demo context: circuit theory). Students authenticate as enrolled learners and access homework through a course dashboard. The experience should feel like part of the course—not a disconnected third-party app.
