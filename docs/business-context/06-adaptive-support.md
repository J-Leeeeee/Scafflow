# Adaptive Support During Problem Solving

Profiles set the **opening move**. Adaptation is what happens **during** the game.

---

## Why Adaptation Matters

Personalization that stops at onboarding is incomplete. Two students on the same profile can diverge within minutes: one flows through mesh analysis; another stalls on sign conventions. Scafflow’s distinctive value is support that **responds to learner state in the moment**.

---

## Learner States (Conceptual)

During problem solving, the system infers states such as:

| State | Plain-language meaning |
|-------|------------------------|
| **Progress** | Moving forward vs. spinning on one step |
| **Engagement** | Active work vs. disengagement or long idle periods |
| **Support uptake** | Using, ignoring, or requesting help |
| **Cognitive effort** | Struggling productively vs. overwhelmed vs. cruising |

These are inferred from **lightweight behavioral signals**—things like time on step, attempts, errors, navigation patterns, and explicit help requests. The product should avoid invasive surveillance; signals must be proportionate, transparent, and privacy-respecting.

---

## How Support Adjusts

When states change, Scafflow may:

- **Add structure** — break the next step into smaller pieces
- **Simplify** — reduce clutter or merge distracting UI
- **Offer guidance** — hints that point toward method, not final answers
- **Fade help** — fewer prompts when the student is succeeding independently
- **Change pacing** — shorter or longer waits before proactive nudges

The student remains in control: they can take offered help, dismiss it, or ask for more.

---

## AI’s Role

AI assistance is a **scaffold**, not an answer key. Hints should:

- Reference the student’s current step and visible work
- Encourage correct reasoning paths
- Avoid dumping solutions that short-circuit learning
- Respect limits so help cannot be infinitely mined for answers

Instructors should be able to set policies on how directive AI help can be.

---

## Productive Struggle vs. Abandonment

The system must distinguish:

- **Productive struggle** — effort, retries, partial progress → measured support, not immediate answers
- **Harmful stuckness** — prolonged idle, repeated errors, help-seeking without uptake → more structure or a different hint style
- **Gaming** — suspiciously fast correct answers with no visible reasoning → integrity safeguards (without punishing legitimately fast experts)

Future enhancements may include optional, privacy-preserving multimodal signals (e.g., engagement cues) **only** with clear consent and institutional approval. These are not required for the core value proposition.

---

## Fading Over Time

Adaptation is bidirectional:

- Struggling → more support
- Succeeding with low help use → less support on subsequent steps and future problems

The long-term goal is **transfer**: students need less scaffolding on the same topic family over the term.
